03 / The buying method

A better shortlist starts with better questions.

For Wheebuy spreadsheet finds, a useful comparison records what you can verify—and leaves room for what you still need to ask.

Keep the product URL, chosen variant, measurements, listed price and unanswered questions together. Recheck the original listing before paying.

Estimate the landed cost

Item price + domestic delivery + service fees + international shipping + conversion charges + applicable taxes or duties.

Identify the exact item

Read the title, description and selected variant. Confirm whether photos show the same color, material and quantity as the option you intend to buy.

Compare measurements

For shoes, check the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance. For clothing, compare garment measurements with something that already fits you.

Inspect the evidence

Look for clear detail photos, fabric information, stitching, hardware and seller terms. A polished photograph alone does not establish quality or authenticity.

Check delivery and returns

Confirm destination eligibility, available shipping methods, return deadlines and any restrictions before committing to an order.

Recalculate before payment

Use the actual checkout currency and current charges. Treat prices, availability and delivery estimates as unconfirmed until the seller or service confirms them.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

Where do the category links lead?

The category shortcuts lead to the specified CAFANS category pages. General browsing links lead to the CAFANS homepage.

How should I compare sneaker and clothing sizes?

For sneakers, compare the listing’s size chart and foot-length guidance. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you already own. A size label alone is not enough.

Are the pictured items verified products available to buy?

No. The images provide visual references only. This page does not verify stock, authenticity, seller affiliation or availability. Check the destination listing and seller information before making a purchase.

What should I include in a total-cost estimate?

Include the item price, domestic delivery, any agent or service fees, international shipping, currency conversion charges and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the actual charges before paying.
